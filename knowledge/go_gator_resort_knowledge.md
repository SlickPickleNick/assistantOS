## 8. General Hotel Policies & Guest Guidelines

- **Standard Check-in**: **4:00 PM**. Guests arriving early may check bags with Bell Services and utilize resort pools, dining, and beaches immediately.
- **Standard Check-out**: **11:00 AM**. Express mobile checkout or in-room TV checkout available.
- **Guaranteed Early Check-in (10:00 AM)**: **$50 flat fee** (subject to advance reservation & inventory availability).
- **Guaranteed Late Check-out (2:00 PM)**: **$50 flat fee** (subject to inventory; complimentary for Coral & Gators tiers; 4:00 PM for Platinum Gators).
- **Financial Guarantee & Incidentals**: Valid government-issued photo ID and credit card required at check-in. An authorization hold of **$150 per night** is placed on the card for incidental charges.

### Reservation Cancellation Rules
- **Standard Guest Rooms (Garden, Resort, Ocean View, Ocean Front)**: Free cancellation up to **5 days prior to arrival date**. Cancellations within 5 days forfeit the first night room rate + tax.
- **Suites & Villas (Junior, Executive, 2-Bedroom Villas)**: Free cancellation up to **14 days prior to arrival date**. Cancellations within 14 days forfeit **50% of the total reservation cost**.
- **No-Show Policy**: Forfeits 100% of the entire stay value.

### Pet Policy
- **Designated Zone**: Permitted **exclusively in select Garden View category rooms** (strictly limited inventory; advance booking mandatory).
- **Fee & Weight Limitations**: Non-refundable **$75 pet cleaning fee per stay**. Maximum **2 pets per room**, with a **50 lb combined weight limit**.
- **Service Animals**: Fully certified ADA service animals exempt from pet fees and room category limitations.

### Smoking & Cleanliness Policy
- **100% Smoke-Free Property**: Smoking, vaping, and e-cigarettes are strictly forbidden in all indoor guest rooms, corridors, dining venues, and private balconies.
- **Designated Smoking Areas**: Marked outdoor zones located adjacent to the multi-level parking structure.
- **Violation Surcharge**: A **$250 deep-cleaning room restoration fee** is charged directly to the guest folio for any violation.

### Age & Safety Policies
- **Primary Registration Age**: Lead guest must be **21 years or older**. Guests aged 18–20 may check in only with a verified parental/guardian financial consent form on file prior to arrival.
- **Adult Pool Access**: Serenity Pool strictly restricted to individuals **18 years of age and older**.

### Payment, Fees & Currencies
- **Accepted Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, and pin-debit cards with sufficient authorized credit balance.
- **Resort Fee & Taxes**: Mandatory, non-waivable, and apply to all promotional, comp, and points rates.
- **Currency**: U.S. Dollars (USD) exclusively. On-site foreign currency exchange available at Front Desk (rates updated daily).

### Lost & Found and Property Damage
- **Lost & Found Retention**: Unclaimed items secured in security vault for **90 calendar days**. Post-departure shipping costs invoiced to guest.
- **Damages**: Damage exceeding standard wear-and-tear assessed and billed to the guest card on file within 5 business days, supported by photographic evidence and itemized receipts.

## 12. Reservation Management & AI Modification Flows

### Common Reservation Scenarios
1. **Reservation Inquiry**: Locate folio via **Confirmation Code + Guest Last Name** or Loyalty Number.
2. **Stay Extension / Add Nights**: Verify seasonal room availability and quote prevailing night rate.
3. **Room Category Upgrade**: Calculate rate differential between booked tier and requested tier; check loyalty status for complimentary eligibility.
4. **Guest Additions**: Update passenger counts; calculate extra person fee ($45 adult, $25 child) if exceeding double occupancy.
5. **Promo Code Application**: Verify validity window and deduct discounted amount.
6. **Reservation Cancellation**: Audit cancellation window (5 days for rooms, 14 days for suites/villas); calculate penalty if inside restricted window.
7. **Folio & Email Resend**: Re-trigger automated confirmation summary to email address on file.

### Required AI Concierge Confirmation Script Sequence
When modifying an existing reservation, the AI Concierge must strictly execute this 5-step flow:
1. **Verify Identity**: Ask for and confirm confirmation number + guest last name (or loyalty account number).
2. **State Current Folio Details**: Clearly summarize arrival date, departure date, room type, and existing guest count.
3. **Quote Variances Clearly**: Explicitly quote any rate adjustments, upgrade differentials, extra person fees, or taxes.
4. **Obtain Explicit Guest Confirmation**: Request affirmative guest agreement before submitting changes.
5. **Execute & Transmit Receipt**: Process the change in the reservation engine and send the updated itinerary receipt email.
